# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: {{PUBLICATION_DATE}}*

## In short

- Your API key is stored encrypted, used only for your own questions, never shown again and deleted when you remove it.
- Your questions go to the AI provider you chose and are private to you. I don't read, review or analyse them.
- I email you about the AIM Framework only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
- I don't sell, rent or trade your information.

## 1. Who I am

aimtheory.org (the **Site**) is run personally by Yule Guttenbeil, an individual based in Victoria, Australia, as a non-commercial research resource. I handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact: info@aimtheory.org.

In this policy, **personal information** means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable, whether or not it is true and whether or not it is recorded in a material form.

## 2. Using the Site without giving personal information

You can read the Site without an account and without giving me any personal information. An account needs only an email address and a display name, which need not be your real name.

## 3. What I collect

- **Account details:** your email address and display name, your sign-in credentials (passwords are stored only in hashed form by Supabase), your sign-up date, and your choices about these documents and about updates.
- **Your API key:** the key itself (encrypted, see section 6), which provider it is for, and whether the provider accepted it.
- **Your questions and answers:** Conversations are stored only so that you can see your own history. You can delete them, and they are deleted when you close your account.
- **Technical information:** server logs (IP address, browser type, time of request and errors), used to keep the Site secure and working and kept for 1 day. These logs do not record API keys or the content of questions.
- **Analytics and cookies:** Vercel Web Analytics counts visits. It identifies a visit with a hash of the request rather than a third-party cookie, and discards that session after 24 hours. Vercel Speed Insights records anonymous loading measurements, including the page, browser, device, country and a web vital. Google Analytics is also used (measurement ID G-8FH4WH9DQH) and sets its own cookies. Sign-in uses essential cookies from Supabase.
- **Where you left off in a chapter:** Opening a treatise chapter again continues from the place you had reached on that page, including a place you opened from a link on the page. A cookie in your browser holds that place. If you are not signed in, I do not add it to any account. If you are signed in, I save it with your account so the same place is there on another device, and I delete it when you close your account.
- **Messages you send me** by email or through the contact form.

## 4. How I collect it

I collect information directly from you when you sign up, save a key or contact me. The only information I receive from your provider is whether it accepted your key. Technical information is collected automatically when you use the Site. You can set your browser to refuse cookies, but you may then be unable to sign in, and a chapter will open at the top.

## 5. How I use it

I use your information to:

- run your account;
- open a treatise chapter where you left off;
- send your requests to your chosen provider;
- keep the Site secure and prevent misuse;
- reply to your messages;
- send emails about your account, security and changes to the Terms of Use or this policy; and
- send updates about the AIM Framework, if you have opted in.

I don't use your questions or answers to develop the framework or to train any model, and I don't use your information for advertising.

The Site does not use automated processes to make decisions that significantly affect your rights or interests. Ask the AI answers your questions; it does not make decisions about you.

## 6. Your API key

Your key is encrypted before it is stored, and the encryption key is held separately from the database, as an environment secret on Vercel and not in the database. Your key is decrypted only in the Site's server memory, at the moment a request is sent to your provider, and it is used only for your own requests. It is never displayed after you save it, never written to logs and never shared. I don't view keys in the ordinary running of the Site.

Because the Site has to decrypt your key to use it, your key is not beyond the reach of the Site's own systems. For that reason, I recommend a spending limit with your provider and a key created only for this Site.

Your key is deleted when you remove it or close your account, including from backups within 7 days.

## 7. Your questions and your AI provider

Each request sends your question, the conversation so far and the relevant Treatise passages to your chosen provider. The provider handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy, which I don't control. Providers' practices differ.

Google's Gemini API uses content sent on its free (unpaid) tier to improve Google's products, and human reviewers may read it. Content sent on Google's paid tier is not used that way. OpenRouter passes requests on to other model providers, and what those providers keep depends on the model you use and your OpenRouter privacy settings.

Please read your provider's terms:

- [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
- [Google Gemini API](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms)
- [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/privacy)

## 8. Sensitive information

I don't ask for sensitive information, such as health information, and I don't knowingly collect it. Anything you include in a question goes to your chosen provider and is stored only for your own history and is not reviewed by me. Please don't include health information or other sensitive personal information in your questions.

## 9. Updates about the AIM Framework

If you tick the updates box at sign-up, or turn updates on in your profile, I record your name and email address in my contact database. I may then email you about the development of the AIM Framework, such as new chapters and papers, research collaboration and events.

Every update email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also turn updates off in your profile or email me. I action unsubscribe requests within five business days, and my emails comply with the *Spam Act 2003* (Cth). Emails about your account, security and changes to the Terms of Use or this policy are sent whether or not you opt in to updates.

## 10. Who else handles your information

- The AI provider you choose, for your requests.
- Service providers who host and run the Site: Vercel in Washington, D.C., United States, Supabase for the database and authentication in Mumbai, India, Resend for email delivery in the United States, and my contact database, which is hosted by Supabase in Sydney, Australia. Each handles information only to provide its service.
- My professional advisers, where needed.
- A successor that takes over the Site under the Terms of Use, which will be bound to handle your information consistently with this policy.
- Authorities, where the law requires it.

## 11. Overseas disclosure

The AI providers process requests mainly in the United States. Vercel (United States), Supabase for the site database and authentication (India), Resend (United States) and Google Analytics (United States) also handle information outside Australia. The contact database is in Sydney, Australia. When you choose a provider, you are directing the Site to send your requests to it.

## 12. Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your information. Information is encrypted in transit, stored keys are encrypted at rest, administrative access is restricted, and logs exclude keys and question content. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

If I become aware that keys or account information have been accessed without authorisation, I will promptly email the affected users and tell them what to do, including whether to revoke their keys. To report a security problem, email info@aimtheory.org.

## 13. Keeping and deleting information

When you close your account, I delete your account details, your stored key, your stored conversations, the places you had reached in the treatise, and your record in my contact database. I keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so that you are not emailed again. Deleted information may remain in backups for up to 7 days before it is overwritten.

When I no longer need personal information and am not required by law to keep it, I take reasonable steps to destroy it or de-identify it.

## 14. Your choices

You can ask me to give you a copy of your information (including in a portable format), correct it, delete it, or stop sending you updates. Email info@aimtheory.org and I will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for an access or correction request. If I refuse a request, I will tell you why.

If you are not satisfied with my response, you can contact the privacy regulator where you live.

## 15. Age

The Site is for people aged 18 and over. If I learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, I will delete it.

## 16. Changes to this policy

The current version and its date will always be on this page. If a change is material, I will email account holders before it takes effect.
